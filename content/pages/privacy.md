---
title: Privacy
updated: 2026-10-01
---

This site has no server of its own. It keeps nothing about you, because it has
nowhere to keep it.

**Signing in.** Signing in with Google lets this site make files in your own
Google Drive and open the files it made. That is the whole of the permission it
asks for. It cannot see, open or list anything else in your Drive, including
files another app made.

**Where your work lives.** Every post and every picture you add is saved in a
folder of your own Google Drive, under your own Google account. It is never
copied to a server belonging to this site, because there is no such server.
Nobody running this site can read it.

**What ever leaves your Drive.** Nothing, until you press Publish. Publishing
shares that one file, and the pictures it points at, so anyone with the link can
read them. Unpublishing takes the sharing away. Deleting a post deletes its
picture from your Drive too.

**What your browser keeps.** The site also keeps a copy of your work in your
browser, so it opens at once and works without a connection. Clearing this
site's data in your browser removes that copy and leaves the one in your Drive
alone.

**The newsletter.** If you give us your email address, that is the one thing
about you this site keeps. It is held by Resend, who send the mail, so that we
can tell you when there is something new here. It is used for nothing else, it
is never sold and never passed on, and every email has a link that takes you off
the list for good. You can also ask us to remove it and we will.

The address goes to a small endpoint of our own rather than straight from the
page, because the key that sends the mail cannot safely live in a web page. That
endpoint keeps no copy of anything.

**What else is collected.** Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking, and no account with anybody but Google.

**Taking it back.** Sign out whenever you like. To withdraw the site's
permission altogether, remove it at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
Your files stay in your Drive, yours to keep or delete like any others.
