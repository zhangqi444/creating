This is a picture blog. Each post is one picture, a title and a date; some carry
a caption or a line or two about how the thing was made.

It is also a blog you can own. Signing in with Google gives you a blog of your
own, kept in **your own Google Drive** — not on a server here, because there
isn't one. Your posts are private until you press Publish, which shares one file
in your Drive so anyone with the link can read it without signing in to
anything. Unpublishing takes that back.

There are no comments, no analytics and no accounts other than Google's. The
[Privacy](#/privacy) page says exactly what that means.

The four posts above came with the repository as placeholders, to show the shape
of a post. Replace them with your own and this page along with them.
