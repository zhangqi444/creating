A circle, a square and a triangle, in that order, because that is the order they
were cut out in. The plan was one shape. The second one went next to it to see
whether two of them still looked like anything, and the third went in to fill the
gap the first two had left at the edge of the paper.

Flat colour, no outlines, and the same grey behind all three so nothing had to
fight for the front. The triangle is the pale one on purpose: it is the biggest,
and in blue it took over the whole page.
